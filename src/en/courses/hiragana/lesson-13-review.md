## Pairs that are easy to mix up

| Pair | How they differ |
|---|---|
| ぬ / め | ぬ has a small loop at the end, め does not |
| ね / れ / わ | ね has a loop, れ flicks outward, わ curls inward |
| る / ろ | る has a loop at the end, ろ does not |
| は / ほ | ほ has an extra horizontal stroke |
| さ / ち | ち looks like the number 5 |

## Rules to remember

- **し** shi, **ち** chi, **つ** tsu, **ふ** fu, **じ** ji.
- The mark **゛** makes a voiced sound and the mark **゜** makes a semi-voiced sound (only on the は row).
- A small **ゃ ゅ ょ** joins the letter before it into one beat: きょ (1 beat) is not きよ (2 beats).
- A long vowel takes an extra beat: おかあさん (5 beats).
- **っ** is a silent beat that doubles the next consonant: がっこう (gakkou).
- **は** is read "wa", **へ** is read "e" and **を** is read "o" when they are particles.
