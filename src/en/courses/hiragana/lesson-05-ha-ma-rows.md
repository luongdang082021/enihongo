## The pattern

**h** + a i u e o and **m** + a i u e o. The one exception is **ふ**, which is read **fu**, not "hu".

## は and へ as particles

In the chart, は is read **ha** and へ is read **he**. When they work as particles inside a sentence, they are read differently: **は is read "wa"** and **へ is read "e"**. You will meet this again in lesson 12. For now, just remember that the writing stays は and へ; only the reading changes.

## Easy to mix up

- **は** and **ほ**: ほ has an extra horizontal stroke.
- **ぬ** and **め**: ぬ has a small loop at the end, め does not.
- **ま** and **も**: ま has a loop at the bottom, も has two bars and a hook.

## Reading practice

Read each word out loud. さむい has 3 beats: sa, mu, i.
