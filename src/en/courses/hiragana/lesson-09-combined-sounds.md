## How they combine

Letters from the い column (き し ち に ひ み り ぎ じ び ぴ) combine with a small **ゃ ゅ ょ** to make one sound. These three small letters look like や ゆ よ but are smaller and sit low and to the right.

For example: **き** (ki) + **ゃ** = **きゃ** (kya). The whole group is read as **one beat**, not as "ki-ya".

## Telling sizes apart

- **きょ** (kyo) is one beat. **きよ** (ki-yo) is two beats. Whether the letter is small or large changes the word.
- In romaji: しゃ = **sha**, しゅ = **shu**, しょ = **sho**, ちゃ = **cha**, じゃ = **ja**. These are not written "sya", "tya" or "zya".

## Reading practice

Read each word out loud. しゃしん has 3 beats: sha, shi, n. おちゃ has 2 beats: o, cha.
