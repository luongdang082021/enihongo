## Two new marks

Adding a mark to a letter you already know gives you a new letter, so there are no new shapes to learn:

- The mark **゛** (two small strokes) makes a **voiced** sound: k becomes g, s becomes z, t becomes d, h becomes b.
- The mark **゜** (a small circle) makes a **semi-voiced** sound, only with the は row: h becomes p.

| Base letters | Add ゛ | Add ゜ |
|---|---|---|
| か き く け こ | が ぎ ぐ げ ご | |
| さ し す せ そ | ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ | |
| た て と | だ で ど | |
| は ひ ふ へ ほ | ば び ぶ べ ぼ | ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ |

## Notes

- **じ** is read **ji**. It is like し (shi) with the voice turned on.
- **ち** and **つ** also have voiced forms, **ぢ** and **づ**. They are rare and sound like じ (ji) and ず (zu). You do not need to learn to write them yet, just recognise them when you see them.
- The mark ゛ sits at the top right of the letter. Do not confuse ゛ with ゜: ゜ appears only on the は row.

## Reading practice

Read each word out loud. ともだち has 4 beats: to, mo, da, chi.
