## The pattern

These two rows follow the pattern you already know: **t** + a i u e o and **n** + a i u e o. Two letters are read differently from what you might guess:

- **ち** is read **chi**, not "ti".
- **つ** is read **tsu**, not "tu". It sounds like "t" and "s" joined together, with unrounded lips.

## Easy to mix up

- **ぬ** and **ね**: both have a loop at the tail. ぬ starts with a horizontal stroke, while ね starts with a vertical stroke on the left.
- **な** and **た**: both contain a plus-sign shape. な has a loop below, while た has an extra こ-like stroke on the right.

## Reading practice

Read each word out loud. つくえ has 3 beats: tsu, ku, e.
