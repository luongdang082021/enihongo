## The last three letters

- **わ** (wa): the last letter of the w row. Only わ remains in this row.
- **を** (wo): in modern Japanese it is used only as a **particle**, and it is read **o**. You will not see を in the middle of an ordinary word.
- **ん** (n): not a syllable with a vowel. ん takes a full **beat** of its own. For example ほん (hon) has 2 beats: ho, n. にほん has 3 beats: ni, ho, n.

## Easy to mix up

- **わ**, **れ** and **ね**: all three have a vertical stroke on the left. わ curls its tail inward, れ flicks it outward, and ね has a loop.
