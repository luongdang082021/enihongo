## Greetings

This lesson uses the letters you have learned to read everyday phrases. A few unusual letters and special readings are explained right below.

- **こんにちは** and **こんばんは**: the final **は** is read **wa**, not "ha". These phrases started out as longer sentences that were shortened, so は still plays the role of a particle.
- **すみません**: the ん at the end of the word is read "n".
- **おやすみなさい**: used when parting in the evening or before going to bed.

## Introducing yourself

The simplest pattern:

> はじめまして。わたしは ___ です。

Read: hajimemashite. watashi wa ___ desu. Meaning: "Nice to meet you. I am ___." Here **は** is read **wa** because it is the topic particle. Fill the blank with your name, for example written in Hiragana.

## Speaking practice

Read the whole sentence out loud 3 times, slowly and then faster. For words with long vowels (おはよう, ありがとう, さようなら), remember to hold the vowel for its full beats.

The mini quiz in this lesson skips the two words with は read "wa", because they need the separate particle rule.
