## The rule

The small **っ** (a tiny tsu) is not read "tsu". It is a **silent beat**: you pause for one beat and then pronounce the consonant that follows.

In romaji, **double the consonant** after っ:

- がっこう: ga + っ + ko + u → **gakkou**
- きって: ki + っ + te → **kitte**
- ざっし: za + っ + shi → **zasshi** (sh becomes ssh)
- みっつ: mi + っ + tsu → **mittsu** (ts becomes tts)
- ちょっと: cho + っ + to → **chotto**

## With and without っ

- **きて** (kite) means "come". **きって** (kitte) means a postage stamp.
- **さか** (saka) means a slope. **さっか** (sakka) means a writer.

Try saying each pair out loud and feel the silent beat in the middle of the second word.

## Counting beats

っ takes one beat. がっこう has 4 beats: ga, っ, ko, u. Each time you read, tap along with the beats to remember.
