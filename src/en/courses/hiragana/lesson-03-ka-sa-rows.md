## The pattern

Each row pairs a consonant with the five vowels you already know: **k** + a i u e o gives か き く け こ, and **s** + a i u e o gives さ し す せ そ.

There is one exception to remember: **し** is read **shi**, not "si". The same sound appears in the word すし (sushi).

## Easy to mix up

- **さ** and **き**: き has a curled last stroke, さ does not.
- **け** and **せ**: same overall shape, but せ has a horizontal stroke that extends to the right.
