## Remember

These five vowels are the foundation of the whole chart: every other letter ends in one of these five sounds (ka, ki, ku, ke, ko...). Say **a, i, u, e, o** in order a few times until it feels natural.

- Japanese vowels are short and crisp. They are not stretched out.
- **う** is pronounced with relaxed lips, not rounded like English "oo".
- **あ** and **お** are easy to mix up. Look for the extra short stroke on the left of お.
