## The pattern

The **y** row has only three letters: **や ゆ よ** (ya, yu, yo). There is no "yi" or "ye".

For the **r** row, lightly tap the tip of your tongue on the roof of your mouth, very quickly. The sound is not quite an English "r" and not quite an "l". If it feels strange, just say "ra, ri, ru, re, ro" lightly and briefly.

## Easy to mix up

- **る** and **ろ**: る has a loop at the end, ろ does not.
- **れ** and **ね**: ね has a loop in the tail, while れ flicks outward.
- **ぬ** and **る**: ぬ has an extra stroke at the front.

## Reading practice

Read each word out loud. さくら has 3 beats: sa, ku, ra.
