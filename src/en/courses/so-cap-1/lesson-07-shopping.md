## これ and この

**これ・それ・あれ** stand alone. **この・その・あの** must be followed by a noun: このほん (this book), そのかばん (that bag), あのくつ (those shoes over there).

## Asking for things

> **〜を ください。** Please give me 〜.

**を** marks the thing the action is aimed at. The letter を is read **o**.

## Expensive and cheap

**たかい** (expensive) and **やすい** (cheap) are adjectives. Add **です** to be polite: このかばんは たかいです.

## The particle で

**ぜんぶで** means "in total". Here **で** marks the range being added up.
