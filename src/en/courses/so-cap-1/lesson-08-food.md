## Verbs in the ます form

Polite verbs end in **ます**. Negatives change to **ません**.

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| たべます (eat) | たべません (do not eat) |

## Sentence pattern

> **Person は food を たべます。**

The verb always comes last. Example: わたしは りんごを たべます.

## Time words

**まいあさ** means "every morning". Time words go before the food and need no particle.

## Note

In ひるごはんに, **に** means "for (lunch)".
