## Means of transport with で

> **Vehicle で Place へ いきます。**

Example: バスで がっこうへ いきます. **で** means "by".

## On foot

Walking is **あるいて**; it does not take で.

## Going with someone

**と** follows the person you go with: ともだちと えきへ いきます. For "alone" use **ひとりで**.

## Go and come

**いきます** is moving away from where you are, **きます** is moving towards where you are.
