## Greetings by time of day

- **おはようございます**: morning. Among friends you can shorten it to おはよう.
- **こんにちは**: daytime. The final **は** is read **wa**.
- **こんばんは**: evening; again **は** is read **wa**.
- **おやすみなさい**: before bed or when parting late at night.

## Thanks, apologies, requests

**ありがとうございます** is the polite form of ありがとう. The **ございます** part makes the phrase more formal.
**すみません** is a light apology, a way to get someone's attention, or to ask to pass. **ごめんなさい** is closer to a sincere apology. **おねがいします** is what you say when asking someone to do something for you.

## At the table

Say **いただきます** before eating and **ごちそうさま** afterwards. Japanese people say both even when eating alone.

## How to read the sentence patterns

In this course each sentence is split into words with spaces so it is easier to read. In normal writing Japanese has no spaces.
