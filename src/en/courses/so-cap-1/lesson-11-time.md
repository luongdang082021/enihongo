## Reading the hours

Number + **じ**: いちじ, にじ, さんじ... Three hours are irregular:

- 4 o'clock = **よじ** (not よんじ)
- 7 o'clock = **しちじ**
- 9 o'clock = **くじ**

## Half past

Add **はん** after the hour: くじはん (9:30).

## Morning and afternoon

Put **ごぜん** before morning times and **ごご** before afternoon and evening times.

## From and until

**〜から** (from) and **〜まで** (until): がっこうは くじから です.
