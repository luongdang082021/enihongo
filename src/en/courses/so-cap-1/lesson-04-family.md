## Two sets of words

Japanese uses one word for people in **your own** family and another for **someone else's** family.

| Person | Own family | Someone else's |
|---|---|---|
| father | ちち | おとうさん |
| mother | はは | おかあさん |
| older brother | あに | おにいさん |
| older sister | あね | おねえさん |

For younger siblings (おとうと, いもうと) the same word is used for your own; for someone else's you add **さん**: おとうとさん, いもうとさん.

## When you address them directly

When you call your own parents directly you also say おとうさん and おかあさん.

## Revisiting の

わたしの はは = my mother. ミンさんの おとうさん = Minh's father.
