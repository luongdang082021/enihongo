## The seven weekdays

Weekday names come from natural elements: **げつ** (moon), **か** (fire), **すい** (water), **もく** (wood), **きん** (gold), **ど** (earth), **にち** (sun), plus **ようび**.

## Today, tomorrow, yesterday

きょう (today), あした (tomorrow), きのう (yesterday). For the past, change **です** to **でした**: きのうは すいようびでした.

## The particle と

After a person **と** means "with": ともだち**と** あいます (meet a friend).

## Asking when

**いつ** is "when": たんじょうびは いつですか.
