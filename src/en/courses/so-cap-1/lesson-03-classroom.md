## Three pointing words

- **これ**: something near the speaker.
- **それ**: something near the listener.
- **あれ**: something far from both.

If you ask "これは なんですか", the other person answers with **それ**, because the object is near you but "that" to them. Always think about where the object is.

## Negative

To say "is not", change **です** to **じゃ ありません**: これは ノートじゃ ありません.

## The particle の

**A の B** means B of A: わたしの かばん (my bag).
