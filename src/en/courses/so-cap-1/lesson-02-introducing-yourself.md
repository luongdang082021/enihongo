## The basic pattern

> **A は B です。** A is B.

**は** is the topic particle, read **wa**. **です** goes at the end to make the sentence polite. Example: わたしは ミンです (I am Minh).

## Making a question with か

Add **か** at the end to make a question; the word order does not change: あなたは せんせいですか (Are you a teacher?).

## Calling other people by name

Add **さん** after someone else's name: ミンさん. Never use さん for yourself.

## The meeting phrases

When you meet someone for the first time say **はじめまして**, introduce yourself, and finish with **よろしくおねがいします**.
