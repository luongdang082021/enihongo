## Three key particles

- **に**: a specific time. ろくじ**に** おきます (get up at six).
- **へ**: direction. がっこう**へ** いきます. Here へ is read **e**.
- **を**: marks the thing acted on. にほんご**を** べんきょうします.

## Parts of the day

**あさ・ひる・よる・まいにち** go before the verb without a particle.

## Word order

The verb always comes last. The other parts can move as long as each keeps its particle.
