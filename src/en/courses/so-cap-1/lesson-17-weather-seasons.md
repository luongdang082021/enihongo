## い-adjectives

Adjectives like **あつい, さむい, すずしい, あたたかい** all end in い. Add **です** to be polite: きょうは あついです.

## Adjective before a noun

Put the adjective right before the noun, nothing extra is needed: **いい てんき** (nice weather).

## The four seasons

はる (spring), なつ (summer), あき (autumn), ふゆ (winter).

## Note

**いい** (good) is an irregular adjective. Its negative becomes よくありません, which we will learn in a later course.
