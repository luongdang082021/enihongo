## The pattern

The **ヤ** row has only three letters: **ya, yu, yo** (the basic chart has no "yi" or "ye"). The **ラ** row is **r** + a i u e o: ラ リ ル レ ロ. The Japanese r sits between English "r", "l" and "d".

## Easy to mix up

- **ル** and **レ**: ル has two strokes like legs, レ has just one.
- **ロ** is a closed square, unlike **コ**, which is open on the left.
- **ユ** and **ヨ**: ユ has two strokes, ヨ has three bars.

## Loanwords

ホテル (hotel), ミルク (milk), タオル (towel), ライス (rice), カメラ (camera), コアラ (koala), タイヤ (tire), アルミ (aluminium). **カメラ** has 3 beats: ka, me, ra.
