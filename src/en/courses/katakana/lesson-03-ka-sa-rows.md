## The pattern

As in Hiragana: **k** + a i u e o gives カ キ ク ケ コ, and **s** + a i u e o gives サ シ ス セ ソ.

One exception to remember: **シ** is read **shi**, not "si".

## Easy to mix up

- **シ** and **ツ** (next lesson) look very alike; lesson 8 teaches how to tell them apart.
- **ク** and **ケ**: ケ is longer and has a bar in the middle.
- **コ** is a square open on the left; no other letter is easily confused with it.

## First loanwords

These sound close to English: アイス (ice), スカイ (sky), ココア (cocoa). **オアシス** has 4 beats: o, a, shi, su.
