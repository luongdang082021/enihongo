## Combined sounds

As in Hiragana, a letter from the **i** column joined with a small **ャ ュ ョ** makes one sound that takes **a single beat**: キ + ャ = **キャ** (kya), シ + ュ = **シュ** (shu), チ + ョ = **チョ** (cho). The small ャ ュ ョ must be written **small**, at the lower right of the letter before. At full size they would make two beats.

Readings: ジャ = **ja**, ジュ = **ju**, ジョ = **jo** (like じゃ じゅ じょ).

## The long-vowel mark ー

Hiragana lengthens a vowel by repeating it. Katakana uses a dedicated mark, a **long horizontal stroke** (in horizontal text) or a **long vertical stroke** (in vertical text): **ー**.

- コーヒー = ko**o**hi**i**: ー extends o, then i.
- ケーキ = ke**e**ki.
- ラーメン = ra**a**men.

Each ー takes **one beat**, so コーヒー has 4 beats: ko, o, hi, i. In romaji, repeat the vowel: *koohii*.

Do not confuse ー with the kanji for one (一) or with a hyphen.

## Loanwords

コーヒー, ケーキ, ジュース, ラーメン, ビール, タクシー, チーズ, ノート, シャワー, チョコレート, キャベツ, ジャム. Say them aloud and count the beats: ジュース = ju, u, su (3 beats).
