## You have come a long way

You have now learned all of Katakana: 46 basic letters, the two marks ゛ ゜, combined sounds, the **ー** mark, small **ッ** and extended sounds. This lesson is for review.

## Tips for reading loanwords

- **It sounds like English, but in Japanese beats.** Each letter is one beat, so "restaurant" becomes レストラン (re, su, to, ra, n).
- Vowels are inserted between consonants: "sports" becomes スポーツ (su, po, o, tsu).
- English words often end with **ー**: "computer" becomes コンピューター and "super" becomes スーパー.
- Not every loanword comes from English: パン (Portuguese), ズボン (French).

## Letters you still mix up

If you still confuse シ and ツ, or ソ and ン, go back to lesson 8 and practise with the flashcards. Letters you miss will come back sooner.

Go to the **Dojo** and mix Hiragana and Katakana in one 20-question run. Select both courses to practise recognising the two scripts together.
