## The last three

- **ワ** is read **wa**.
- **ヲ** is read like **o** (romanised wo). In practice ヲ is rarely seen outside old-style names; the particle を is still written in Hiragana.
- **ン** is read **n** and takes **a beat of its own**. ランチ has 3 beats: ra, n, chi.

## Easy to mix up

- **ワ** resembles **ク**; **ン** resembles **ソ** and **シ**. Lesson 8 looks at them closely.

## Loanwords

ワイン (wine), ランチ (lunch), レモン (lemon), メロン (melon), ライオン (lion), インク (ink), ヒント (hint). For ン the mouth closes at the end, like the final "n" in "ban".
