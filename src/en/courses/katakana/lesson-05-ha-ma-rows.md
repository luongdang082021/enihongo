## The pattern

**h** + a i u e o gives ハ ヒ フ ヘ ホ, and **m** + a i u e o gives マ ミ ム メ モ. Exception: **フ** is read **fu**, a sound between "hu" and "fu".

## Easy to mix up

- Katakana **ヘ** is almost identical to Hiragana **へ**, only slightly sharper.
- **メ** (two crossing diagonals) is very different from **ナ**, but can be mistaken for **ノ** when written carelessly. Lesson 8 collects these pairs.
- **ミ** is three short stacked strokes, like a number 3 on its side.

## Loanwords

メモ (memo), ハム (ham), ミス (mistake), マスク (mask), トマト (tomato), ヘア (hair). **スマホ** is the short form of "smartphone", 3 beats: su, ma, ho.
