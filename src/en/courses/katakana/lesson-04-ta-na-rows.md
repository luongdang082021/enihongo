## The pattern

**t** + a i u e o gives タ チ ツ テ ト, and **n** + a i u e o gives ナ ニ ヌ ネ ノ. Two exceptions: **チ** is read **chi** and **ツ** is read **tsu**.

## Easy to mix up

- **ツ** looks like **シ** from the last lesson. For now remember: ツ has two short strokes side by side and a long stroke pulled down.
- **ナ** is a bar crossed by a slanting stroke; **ヌ** has a fold.
- **ノ** is a single slanting stroke: easy to remember, but easy to mistake for a piece of another letter.

## Loanwords

テスト (test), テニス (tennis), ナイス (nice), ツナ (tuna), タイ (Thailand). **ネクタイ** (necktie) has 4 beats: ne, ku, ta, i.
